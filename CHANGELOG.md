# OKS Forever Tactics

## 0.7.0
- All 9 new WoW Forever dungeons: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, and five more, marked "new" in the list.
- Short tank, healer and dps tactics for 217 bosses in 30 dungeons, written by us after reading the WoW Forever dungeon journal.
- 38 more bosses and rares, including the Ring of the Law, the Chest of the Seven, the Atal'ai Defenders and the Lost Dwarves.
- Ability lists and portraits for most bosses in the new dungeons.
- Clicking the dungeon that is already open closes it.

## 0.5.0 - 0.6.0
- All 19 classic 5-player dungeons with every boss, rare and trash mob.
- Health, armor, resistances, immunities, ability timers, health triggers (like enrage at 30%) and flee points.
- Portraits for bosses and mobs, and abilities shown as icons with tooltips.

## 0.1.0 - 0.4.0
- Journal window (`/ft`) and a book button on the target frame that opens the page for your target.
- During a boss fight the button opens that boss; inside a dungeon it guesses the mob from its level and type.
