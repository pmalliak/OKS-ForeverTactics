# OKS Forever Tactics

## 0.8.0
- New look matching OKS Writ Shopper: standard window, tabs, striped rows and a summary bar with health, armor, melee hit and more.
- Easier to read boss pages: tactics first, clear section headers, bigger ability icons and role cards for tank, healer and dps.
- Search box: type part of a mob's name to find it in every dungeon.
- Dungeon pages list bosses and rares as clickable rows.
- Options tab: show or hide the target button, lock its position, reset it, and pick the text size (small, normal, large).
- Loot for every mob in the world: hold Ctrl over a mob to see its drops and drop chances in the tooltip. The key can be changed or set to always in Options.
- Every journal page has a Loot section. In dungeons the game hides which mob you hover, so use the journal there. During a boss fight the target button tooltip shows the boss loot.
- Hold Shift as well to see the whole list in the tooltip. `/ft loot` opens a loot window for your target or the current boss, `/ft loot <name>` for any mob, and a right click on the target button does the same.

## 0.7.1
- First CurseForge release. The version shown in `/ft help` now comes from the release.
- Releases are tagged automatically.

## 0.7.0
- All 9 new WoW Forever dungeons: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, and five more, marked "new" in the list.
- Short tank, healer and dps tactics for 217 bosses in 30 dungeons, written by us after reading the WoW Forever dungeon journal.
- 38 more bosses and rares, including the Ring of the Law, the Chest of the Seven, the Atal'ai Defenders and the Lost Dwarves.
- Ability lists and portraits for most bosses in the new dungeons.
- Clicking the dungeon that is already open closes it.

## 0.5.0 - 0.6.0
- All 19 classic 5-player dungeons with every boss, rare and trash mob.
- Health, armor, resistances, immunities, ability timers, health triggers (like enrage at 30%) and flee points.
- Portraits for bosses and mobs, and abilities shown as icons with tooltips.

## 0.1.0 - 0.4.0
- Journal window (`/ft`) and a book button on the target frame that opens the page for your target.
- During a boss fight the button opens that boss; inside a dungeon it guesses the mob from its level and type.
